How to play

Choose a difficulty and guess one letter at a time. Every correct guess reveals every matching position. Six wrong letters end the round. A loss or surrender ends your streak; switching difficulties starts a new streak when you win. Refreshing resumes your game. Each verified win earns 1, 2 or 3 Star Coins.

Words are rated by letter rarity, distinct letters, traditional vowels, familiarity and common letter patterns, not length alone. Word data: Datamuse.
