How to play

Move the basket using ← and →, A and D, the numbered lane buttons, or a mouse/touch on the orchard. Missing a fresh apple or catching a rotten apple costs one life. Avoid rotten apples. The game gets faster over time. Your coins save automatically during the run; keep this page open until the final save confirms. Press Esc to leave fullscreen.
