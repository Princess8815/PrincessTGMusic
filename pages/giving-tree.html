How to play

Plant your starter 1 attack / 1 defense pear in a free patch. Water trees for free. Fertilizer costs 5 Star Coins per five minutes of boost, stacks, and makes growth four times faster while the tree is watered. Trees progress from seedling to sapling, young tree, then fully grown. Stronger genetics take longer to grow. Mature trees produce pears with slightly varied stats and award one Star Coin per fruit; production pauses when your inventory is full. Start with 40 slots and permanently expand your inventory with Star Coins. Each expansion adds 10 slots, up to 1,000 slots. The first costs 25 Star Coins; each subsequent upgrade costs 15 more. Pear attack and defense vary independently by up to 0.50 in either direction, with a minimum of 1.00 and two decimal places of precision.

Plant a new pear to inherit its attack and defense. Only fully grown trees may be cut down. Click Cut tree once, then click the red Are you sure? button to confirm. Confirmation expires after eight seconds. Discard uses the same in-place confirmation. Enemies unlock at tree attack or defense 2, bosses at 5. Select a pear, then throw it at an opponent. Every throw consumes it. If the pear's defense is lower than the opponent's attack, or its attack is not greater than opponent defense, it deals zero damage. Otherwise damage equals pear attack minus opponent defense. Bosses fully heal 30 seconds after your first throw if still alive. Opponents never attack your trees or you. Enemies and bosses keep increasing in difficulty.

Your progress saves to your account. Trees grow offline while supplied with water and inventory space. Fractional battle rewards accumulate internally; your wallet receives whole coins. If you have no trees and no pears, a free starter seed prevents a dead end.
