How to play

Plant your starter 1 attack / 1 defense pear in a free patch. Water trees for free. Fertilizer costs 5 Star Coins per five minutes of boost, stacks, and makes growth four times faster while the tree is watered. Trees progress from seedling to sapling, young tree, then fully grown. Stronger genetics take longer to grow. Mature trees produce pears with slightly varied stats and award one Star Coin per fruit; production pauses when your inventory is full. Start with 40 slots and permanently expand your inventory with Star Coins. Each expansion adds 10 slots, up to 1,000 slots. The first costs 25 Star Coins; each subsequent upgrade costs 15 more. Pear attack and defense vary independently by up to 0.50 in either direction, with a minimum of 1.00 and two decimal places of precision.

Plant a new pear to inherit its attack and defense. Only fully grown trees may be cut down. Click Cut tree once, then click the red Are you sure? button to confirm. Confirmation expires after eight seconds. Click several pears to select them for bulk discard, or use Clear all to empty the visible inventory. Both use the same red in-place confirmation. Deselect all clears the selection without deleting fruit. Sort by attack, defense, combined stats or newest. Manual planting and throwing require exactly one selected pear. On mobile, the Pears tab also shows quick attacks and planting: use your selected pear, or the first pear in the current sort order if none is selected. Trees and Battle offer explicit first-pear buttons. Every action still uses one pear at a time. Enemies unlock at tree attack or defense 2, bosses at 5. Select a pear, then throw it at an opponent. Every throw consumes it. If the pear's defense is lower than the opponent's attack, or its attack is not greater than opponent defense, it deals zero damage. Otherwise damage equals pear attack minus opponent defense. Bosses fully heal 30 seconds after your first throw if still alive. Opponents never attack your trees or you. Enemies and bosses keep increasing in difficulty.

Pears appear as they grow, and can be planted, thrown or discarded immediately. Your Star Coin wallet and collectible awards update when the server confirms a save. Your progress saves to your account. Trees grow offline while supplied with water and inventory space. Fractional battle rewards accumulate internally; your wallet receives whole coins. If you have no trees and no pears, a free starter seed prevents a dead end.
