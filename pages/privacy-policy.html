Who we are

PrincessTG Creations operates this music, creative-writing, member-services and physical merchandise website from Wisconsin, United States. This notice explains how we handle information when you browse, create an account, use our tools, submit content or shop. Contact princesstgcreations@yahoo.com for privacy questions.

Information you provide

  • Accounts: display name, email, account identifier, sign-in provider, verification status, profile information and subscription preferences. Google sign-in may provide basic profile information. Firebase Authentication handles sign-in credentials; we do not store plaintext passwords in our site database.
  • Creative features: challenge entries, submitted songs or writing, related links, contact details and correspondence you choose to submit; saved playlists and collectible discoveries.
  • Documents: locally stored drafts, saved cloud documents, content, formatting, embedded images and sharing settings.
  • Site Mail: sender and recipient identifiers and display names, subject, message, attachments, read status and delivery/expiry information. Verified members can search the member directory by display name; email addresses are not shown by that directory.
  • Health Tracker: optional food and calorie logs, weight, workouts, meal plans, notes, goals and calculated summaries. Do not enter information about another person without permission.
  • Orders: name, email, shipping address, selected CDs, quantities, prices, shipping, tax, payment reference, status and account identifier when applicable. Stripe collects payment credentials directly through its payment interface; our order service stores payment references, not full card numbers or security codes.

Device and browser information

Our hosting and service providers may process IP addresses, request information, browser/device details and security or diagnostic logs. Browser storage (including local storage, session storage and IndexedDB) supports theme and accessibility preferences, sign-in persistence, local drafts, playlists, carts, pending checkout references and collectibles. Some providers may use cookies or similar technologies for authentication, security or payment features. Clearing site data can remove local drafts and prevent recovery of an unfinished checkout or guest collectible claim. Export anything important first.

How information is used

We use information to operate accounts, save requested content, calculate health summaries, deliver messages and newsletters, administer submissions, protect the site from abuse, diagnose problems, process orders and taxes, arrange shipment, handle customer support and disputes, and meet legal obligations. We do not use saved health journals for advertising or send them to Stripe or Formspree as part of checkout. Health records are intended to be accessible only through their owner's authenticated account, not through ordinary staff or other member access. Infrastructure administrators and service providers may have privileged access needed to operate or secure the service.

Public content and sharing

Publishing a document makes its shared copy public. An unlisted document is accessible to anyone who obtains its link; it is not a private password-protected document. You can revoke the site's shared copy, but cannot recall copies already downloaded or forwarded. Content intentionally submitted for public music, writing or challenges may be displayed according to the submission instructions. Site Mail sends a separate copy to the recipient; deleting your copy does not delete theirs. Avoid including private health, financial or identity information in public content or messages.

Service providers and disclosures

  • Google Firebase provides authentication, cloud storage and related services.
  • GitHub and Cloudflare provide hosting and infrastructure; Cloudflare Workers and D1 process and store order information.
  • Stripe processes payment, authentication, tax calculations and transaction records; checkout sends relevant address and order details to Stripe.
  • Formspree receives creative/contact form submissions and verified order details and forwards notifications to the site operator.
  • When newsletters or promotions are enabled, Resend may receive recipient addresses and message details to deliver opted-in mail.
  • Shipping carriers receive information necessary to deliver physical orders. Social links, music platforms, external images/icons and other external services have their own privacy practices; opening or loading their content can disclose network information to those providers.

Information may also be disclosed when necessary to comply with law, protect rights or safety, investigate abuse, or resolve disputes. We do not offer personal information for sale. Any future advertising or additional data use requires a separate review and updated notice before introduction.

Retention and your choices

Saved records generally remain until you delete them, close your account, or they are no longer needed. Health Tracker offers export and deletion controls. Documents can be exported, deleted and unshared; local copies must be removed from each device separately. Mail is designed to expire after about 30 days; actual removal from storage depends on server cleanup configuration. You can change newsletter and promotion preferences in your profile, or use unsubscribe links where provided.

Account closure attempts to delete saved health records, cloud documents and shares, account playlists and collectibles, then removes the Firebase login. An archived account record, submissions, other recipients' mail, order/tax records, provider logs and backups may remain. Some account types or account states require support-assisted closure. Orders, including incomplete or canceled checkouts, may retain addresses and transaction information in D1 and service-provider systems. Order retention is subject to fulfillment, tax, fraud prevention, disputes and legal obligations; there is not yet an automated order-record deletion schedule. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of eligible information, or to exercise applicable privacy rights. We may verify your identity before fulfilling a request.

Security and health information

We use encrypted connections, Firebase authentication and access-control rules, server-side payment verification and limited-access service credentials to help protect information. No online system is completely secure. The Health Tracker is an optional personal journal, not a healthcare service or an emergency monitoring system. Do not assume HIPAA coverage. We will assess suspected security incidents and provide notifications when required by applicable law.

Children, geographic scope and updates

The site is not intended for children under 13 to provide personal information. If you believe a child under 13 has submitted information, contact us so we can review and remove it as appropriate. Services are operated in the United States; providers may process information in other jurisdictions. We will update this notice when our practices change and identify the date of the update. We will give additional notice or seek consent when required by law.

Questions or accessibility requests? Contact princesstgcreations@yahoo.com.
