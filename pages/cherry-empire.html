How to play

Harvest ripe cherries to buy tree upgrades and plant more trees. New trees take time to mature. Auto harvesters collect fruit even while you are offline. Without an auto harvester, ripe fruit waits on the tree up to its capacity. Upgrade capacity to store more fruit before harvesting. Yield upgrades grow more fruit per cycle and use one slot per fruit; prestige multiplies harvested cherries without using additional slots. If only a few slots remain, growth fills those slots.

Max out fruit speed, cherry yield, auto harvester and capacity to prestige an individual tree. It starts growing again, loses regular upgrades, produces more cherries per fruit and unlocks higher upgrade limits. Permanent Star Coin chance and multiplier upgrades survive prestige and have no level cap. Chance gains diminish above 10%; multiplier bonuses gradually diminish. Fractional Star Coins carry over across trees and harvests until they form whole coins for your wallet.

Growth is calculated from saved timestamps. Your on-screen orchard updates locally; rewards are confirmed by the server during harvests, purchases and automatic saves. You can close the page and return later.
