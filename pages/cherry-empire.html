How to play

Harvest ripe cherries to buy tree upgrades and plant more trees. New trees take time to mature. Auto harvesters collect fruit even while you are offline. Without an auto harvester, ripe fruit waits on the tree for you.

Max out fruit speed and cherry yield to prestige an individual tree. It starts growing again, loses regular upgrades, produces more cherries per fruit and unlocks higher upgrade limits. Permanent Star Coin chance upgrades survive prestige.

Growth is calculated from saved timestamps. Your on-screen orchard updates locally; rewards are confirmed by the server during harvests, purchases and automatic saves. You can close the page and return later.
