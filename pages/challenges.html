Challenges

  • THE FORBIDDEN WORDS CHALLENGE - Write an emotional song without using 10 common emotional words, such as love, heart, pain, tears, broken, alone, fear, lost, cry, or hurt.
  • THE REVERSE STORY CHALLENGE - Start with the ending of a story and work backward through the verses until the listener discovers what caused everything.
  • THE GENRE ROULETTE CHALLENGE - Randomly select two completely different genres and combine them into one song.
  • THE FOUR PERSPECTIVES CHALLENGE - Write four verses about the same event, each from a different person's perspective. Nobody agrees about what happened.
  • THE EMOTIONAL CONTRADICTION CHALLENGE - Write a song with cheerful, upbeat instrumentation but devastating lyrics, or aggressive metal about forgiveness and compassion.
  • THE ONE OBJECT CHALLENGE - Someone chooses an ordinary object. Build an entire emotional narrative around it.
  • THE UNRELIABLE NARRATOR CHALLENGE - Write a song where the narrator believes they're the hero, but the listener gradually realizes they're actually the villain.
  • THE CHANGING CHORUS CHALLENGE - Every chorus must change meaning as the story progresses. The final chorus completely transforms how the listener interprets the first.
  • THE FIVE RANDOM WORDS CHALLENGE - Have five people each contribute one unrelated word. All five must become essential to the story.
  • THE MUSICAL PLOT TWIST CHALLENGE - Write a song that sounds like a romantic love song for the first two verses, but the bridge reveals an entirely different situation.
  • THE ACCIDENTAL SONGWRITER CHALLENGE - Answer 12 questions to create a unique, original song. Try it now!
