The future of PrincessTGMusic

Development Roadmap

From sharing my own songs to building a home for music lovers and independent artists. Here's what I'm hoping to bring to PrincessTGMusic, one feature at a time.

A work in progress: Everything below is planned, not a promise of a release date. Features and priorities may change as I build, test, and improve the website. Statuses shown here are planning placeholders, not live progress updates.

Phase 01

Better Music Library

Planned

Find your next favorite song with easier browsing and more ways to explore.

See planned features
  • Improved song and album pages
  • Artist information and dedicated artist collections
  • Browse and filter by genre, artist, and uploader
  • A smoother experience on phones and computers
Phase 02

Music Releases & Previews

Planned

Discover upcoming singles and albums before their official release.

See planned features
  • Upcoming release announcements and information
  • Audio previews for unreleased songs
  • Release dates and album details
  • Preview players automatically removed from song details when the single or album is released
Phase 03

Paid Memberships

Planned

Optional membership benefits for listeners who want to support the website.

See planned features
  • Ad-free listening
  • Full on-demand music player for members
  • Premium radio options
  • Personalized listening
  • Priority support messages
  • Free visitors can still explore songs and listen to public radio
Phase 04

Weekly Newsletters

Planned

Get updates about new songs, upcoming releases, and the website's progress.

See planned features
  • Optional email subscriptions
  • Weekly updates and featured music
  • Release announcements and occasional special messages
  • Easy unsubscribe option
Phase 05

Contact & Support

Planned

Reach out with questions, feedback, and support requests directly on the website.

See planned features
  • Built-in contact form
  • Organized messages and reply history
  • Priority handling for paid members
  • Spam and harassment protection

Phase 07

Community Music Submissions

Planned

Give independent artists a chance to share their original songs with the community.

See planned features
  • Submit original songs and cover artwork
  • Artist pages and music collections
  • Browse by artist or uploader
  • Hybrid moderation using automated checks and human review where needed
  • Community reporting and takedown tools
  • Submission limits to protect website storage
Phase 08

PrincessTG Radio

Planned

Tune into synchronized stations. Everyone on the same station hears the same music at approximately the same time.

See planned features
  • Metal, Pop, Rock, International, and All Music stations
  • Separate music schedules for each genre
  • Continuous listening and new music discovery
  • More stations as the music library grows

Planned

More control over your music experience with optional membership features.

See planned features

Planned

A Message from PrincessTG

PrincessTGMusic began as a way to share my music, and I have bigger hopes for what it can become. I want to create a welcoming place where listeners discover new music, independent artists share their creativity, and everyone can see what's coming next.
