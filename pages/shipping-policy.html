Where we ship

Available countries, states or provinces and cities are displayed during checkout. If your destination is not listed, contact princesstgcreations@yahoo.com before ordering. Test-mode checkout is for simulation only and does not create a shipment.

Shipping charges and tax

Shipping is calculated from the destination and quantity of CDs using our configured shipping rates, not live carrier quotes. Review the final shipping charge, applicable sales tax and total in USD before submitting payment. If international shipping is offered, any additional duties or import charges will be disclosed where known; local rules may apply.

Processing and shipment timing

Orders are processed manually after successful payment verification. We aim to ship within the timeframe stated for your order or, when no specific timeframe is stated, within 30 days of receiving a properly completed order. This refers to dispatch, not delivery. If we cannot ship within that period, we will notify you, give you the option to agree to a delay or cancel, and provide a prompt refund if required. We will not treat a failure to respond as consent except where legally permitted.

Delivery and address changes

Enter a complete and accurate shipping address, including apartment or unit information. Contact us with your order reference as soon as possible if the address needs correction. We cannot guarantee changes after dispatch. Carrier delivery times vary; tracking details will be supplied when available. Contact us about missing, delayed or damaged orders so we can investigate and arrange an appropriate resolution.

Order status and support

Keep your order reference from the confirmation screen. Contact princesstgcreations@yahoo.com for shipping questions, tracking where available, or cancellation. See the Refund & Return Policy for returns and refunds.

Questions or accessibility requests? Contact princesstgcreations@yahoo.com.
