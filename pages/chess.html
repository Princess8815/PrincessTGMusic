    Rules

    Normal piece movement, promotion, en passant and castling. Checks are warnings only: the king may be exposed and captured. No available move means defeat. At 100 quiet half-moves or 400 total plies, material and mobility decide the winner to prevent endless games. The final tie goes to the last mover. Local practice does not change account Elo or grant collectibles or Star Coins. Rated games award capture coins and win bonuses after completion, scaled by difficulty and membership. Resigning forfeits pending capture coins. Online PvP is planned.
