    Rules

    Normal chess movement, check, promotion, en passant and castling rules apply. You cannot expose your king to check, move into check, or castle through check. Checkmate and stalemate both count as a victory and virtual king capture. At 100 quiet half-moves or 400 total plies, material and mobility decide the winner to prevent endless games. The final tie goes to the last mover. Local practice does not change account Elo or grant collectibles or Star Coins. Rated games award capture coins and win bonuses after completion, scaled by difficulty and membership. Resigning forfeits pending capture coins. Online PvP is planned.
