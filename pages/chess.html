    Rules

    Normal piece movement, promotion, en passant and castling. Checks are warnings only: the king may be exposed and captured. No available move means defeat. At 100 quiet half-moves or 400 total plies, material and mobility decide the winner to prevent endless games. The final tie goes to the last mover. Local practice does not change account Elo or grant collectibles. Online PvP is planned.
