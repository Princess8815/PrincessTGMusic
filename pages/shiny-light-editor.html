Tile palette

Click a cell to place or replace a tile. Stars and coin are overlays. Select Erase to clear a tile, star or coin. Use Inspect to read an existing tile's settings.

Source intensity is 1–9. A mirror's slash alternates with orientation. One-way mirrors reflect only the configured incoming travel direction. Rotating portals turn the beam by the difference between entry and exit orientations. A combiner emits in its configured direction when at least two incoming directions meet. Clear glass walls transmit light but are solid to sliding pieces and ice. In local test mode, tap mirrors to rotate and drag movable pieces.

Feature unlock levels

Change the campaign unlock number of each tile. Source, fruit and basic mirror must stay at level 1. Published levels must respect the current schedule.
