Easy at-home workout ideas

Choose comfortable movement. Start gently, adapt for mobility, and stop if you feel dizzy, faint, or in pain. These suggestions aren't a substitute for medical advice.

Chair mobility

5–10 minutes

  1. Seated shoulder rolls, 30 seconds.
  2. Slow seated arm reaches, 5 per side.
  3. Gentle ankle circles if comfortable.
  4. Pause and breathe between movements.

Gentle strength

8–15 minutes

  1. Supported wall push-ups, 5–8 repetitions.
  2. Seated resistance-band pulls, 5–8 repetitions.
  3. Rest, then repeat if comfortable.

Indoor cardio

5–20 minutes

  1. Easy-paced walking or stationary cycling.
  2. Keep a pace where conversation is comfortable.
  3. Gradually slow down for a cool-down.

Calm stretch routine

5–10 minutes

  1. Gentle shoulder stretches.
  2. Slow neck range-of-motion, pain-free.
  3. Comfortable upper-body stretches.
  4. Finish with quiet breathing.

Food estimates and graphs are planning aids, not medical diagnoses. Missing records are never counted as zero-calorie days.
