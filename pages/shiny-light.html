How to play

Every banana must receive light at the same time. Tap or click a mirror once to rotate it. Drag pieces marked with arrows along their permitted axis; keyboard users can focus a piece and press an arrow key. Only light can push ice. Solid walls block both light and pieces; clear glass walls let light through but stop pieces and ice. Stars and coins are collected by light, not by moving pieces onto them. The markers do not block sliding pieces.

Clear light starts without RGB components. Red + green makes yellow; red + blue makes magenta; green + blue makes cyan. Color doors and colored bananas need the exact color.

Black holes reset the board. Buttons latch until retry; continuous switches must stay lit.

First clears award 10 Star Coins; map coins award one each, once per account and level. Replay to improve your star record. Reset clears campaign progress and stars, not wallet or collectibles.
