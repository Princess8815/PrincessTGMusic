Refund & Return Policy

How to request cancellation, returns and refunds.

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Privacy Policy Terms of Service Refund & Return Policy Shipping Policy

Contact us about an order

Email princesstgcreations@yahoo.com with your order reference, the affected CD and a description of the issue. Do not send full payment card details. This policy applies to real physical CD purchases; test payments do not create shipments or real refunds.

Cancellation before shipping

Contact us promptly if you need to cancel or correct an order. If the order has not shipped, we will cancel it and issue a full refund when requested. If it has shipped, the return process applies. A payment canceled before completion is not a paid order.

Damaged, incorrect or defective CDs

If your CD arrives damaged, defective or incorrect, contact us as soon as reasonably possible. Include the order reference and, if available, photos of the damage or incorrect item. We will arrange an appropriate replacement or refund and cover reasonable return shipping where a return is required for our error or a defective item. Your statutory rights are not limited by this policy.

Change-of-mind returns

You may request a change-of-mind return within 30 days of delivery for an unused CD in its original condition. Contact us before mailing it so we can provide return instructions. For eligible voluntary returns, the customer pays return postage and the original delivery charge is normally not refundable, except where required by law. We will refund the eligible merchandise price and any associated refundable tax after receipt and inspection. Contact us if packaging was opened to discover a defect; this does not waive your rights for faulty merchandise.

Refund processing

Refunds are handled manually through Stripe to the original payment method where possible. We will confirm the refund and initiate it promptly once eligibility is established; the bank or payment provider controls when the credit appears. For orders we cannot ship within the promised period, see our Shipping Policy for delay and cancellation rights. We will not require a return of goods that were never delivered before issuing a legally required refund.

Digital purchases

For a prepaid membership or Star Coin pack, contact support with the order reference or Stripe receipt if the purchase was incorrect, unauthorized, not delivered or you wish to request a refund. Digital refunds are reviewed individually, including the remaining membership time and whether coins have been spent. We will honor any mandatory refund or cancellation rights. A refunded or disputed payment may require account credit review; contact support if you see a discrepancy.

Purchase collectibles

Virtual purchase rewards are subject to verified qualifying orders. Please contact support if a refund affects your collectible claim. Your right to a refund is not conditional on returning or forfeiting a virtual collectible.

Questions or accessibility requests? Contact princesstgcreations@yahoo.com.
