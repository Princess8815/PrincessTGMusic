How to play

Pull Marina backwards from the slingshot and release, or adjust angle and power and press Launch. Hit oranges directly or break load-bearing supports to collapse whole structures. Later levels introduce glass, two-hit brick walls, stone, three-hit steel, and purple reflective barriers. Aim over walls or bank shots off reflectors. Discover spring bumpers, reinforced alloy, and chain-reaction crates as you progress. The feature collection reveals new mechanics in color after you clear their required levels. Aqualily splits into three, Bulky breaks tougher branches, Sploder makes a harmless burst, and Sniper never misses a selected orange. Special throws are extra to Marina's level allowance and carry between levels. Each newly cleared level earns 10 Star Coins. Retry replenishes Marina and resets the orchard, but does not refund used specials. All axolotls bounce safely home.
