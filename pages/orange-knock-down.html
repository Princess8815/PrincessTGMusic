How to play

Pull Marina backwards from the slingshot and release, or adjust angle and power and press Launch. Hit oranges directly or break load-bearing supports to collapse whole structures. Later levels introduce glass, two-hit brick walls, stone, three-hit steel, and purple reflective barriers. Aim over walls or bank shots off reflectors. Discover spring bumpers, angled trampolines that reflect and boost shots, reinforced alloy, and chain-reaction crates as you progress. The feature collection reveals new mechanics in color after you clear their required levels. Aqualily splits into three, Bulky breaks tougher branches, Sploder makes a harmless burst, and Sniper never misses a selected orange. Special throws carry between levels and must be used before Marina runs out. If your final Marina throw leaves any oranges, the level ends. Choose your launcher from the bottom of the fullscreen arena. Tap an orange when using Sniper. Return with Back to buy additional special throws. Each newly cleared level earns 10 Star Coins. Retry replenishes Marina and resets the orchard, but does not refund used specials. All axolotls bounce safely home.
