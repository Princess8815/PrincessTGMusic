How to play

Click or tap peaches as they ripen. The circular meter fills while they grow. Pick when the ring turns gold for 5 points; close timing gives 1–4 points. Picking too early gives zero and removes the fruit. Overripe peaches eventually disappear. Blue peaches double their points. Each perfect pick earns 2 Star Coins; any earlier pick earns 1, even if too early for points. Overripe picks earn none. Blue peaches do not double coins. Paid membership doubles verified coin rewards when the round is credited. During play the counter shows the base amount; the final result shows the actual amount. You have 30 seconds. Peach Panic Mastery requires 240 points in one round. Your result saves to your account at the end.
