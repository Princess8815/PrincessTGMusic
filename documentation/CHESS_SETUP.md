# Chess and board games

Chess uses normal legal moves, including en passant, promotion, castling restrictions, and check protection. Kings cannot enter or remain in check, and are never physically captured. Checkmate and stalemate both count as a victory and a virtual king capture. The game differs from FIDE chess in stalemate and move-limit adjudication. To prevent endless matches, 100 quiet half-moves or 400 total plies trigger material then mobility adjudication (last mover wins a tie). The ten levels use a seeded, bounded JavaScript search; level 1 plays random moves. Bot Elo values are estimated starting values, updated per account by rated PvB matches, not verified FIDE ratings. Online PvP Elo update logic exists but networking is not implemented.

Single elimination: eight entrants (you + seven distinct owned game-earned axolotls), three rounds to win. 3GG: every entrant plays at least three games, with winners and losers bracket feeds, a championship, and a reset final if the undefeated finalist loses game 16. A player losing their opening two games still has a third game in the championship path. The bracket can contain 16 or 17 matches; see [eight-team 3GG reference](https://www.printyourbrackets.com/3-game-guarantee/8-team-seeded-3-game-guarantee.html). Each pending bot match can be watched as actual server-validated chess, one move at a time, or simulated instantly using the seeded equal-rating outcome model. The bracket waits until every match in its current wave is complete. White, Black or Random may be selected in ordinary PvB only. Tournament colors are assigned server-side when each pairing is formed, favoring color balance with a deterministic seeded tie-break. Bracket entries show assigned White and Black; the championship reset reverses the previous final's colors. Existing in-progress boards retain their original color. Future pairings are assigned once opponents are known; tournament difficulty remains independently selected from 1–10. Watched matches preserve their board on the server and do not affect the player's Elo. Local PvP, PvB and BvB practice do not award collectibles or change account ratings. Signed-in rated PvB and tournaments are verified by the live Worker, and a saved active tournament resumes. Rated matches now earn base capture coins: pawn 1, knight/bishop 3, rook 5, queen 9; a checkmate or stalemate adds a virtual king capture worth 10 plus the win reward of 20 (30 total base before other captures); tournament championship 50. Ordinary capture earnings are capped at 60 per game (the virtual king reward is separate) and credited when a match ends naturally, including a loss. Resigning forfeits pending capture earnings. Paid membership doubles the verified payout at credit time, using the matching preview/live staff override. Bot-only and local practice matches never award coins. All game rewards are server-only; the existing privateGames and axolotl rules already prevent client grants. The six collectible unlock rules are in chess-core.mjs; Chess Master requires a difficulty-10 tournament championship.

Test local practice without a Worker. For rated play, after reviewing and testing the current branch, deploy the live Worker using `cd workers/cd-store` then `npx wrangler deploy --config wrangler.live.toml`. Existing verified accounts on approved localhost/preview origins can use it. No new Firestore rules or Blaze needed. Run `node --test workers/cd-store/tests/chess.test.mjs` before deployment. Browser and Cloudflare CPU timing require real testing; high bot levels use bounded search, not Stockfish.

Difficulty scaling: base earnings are multiplied by (difficulty + 3) / 4, rounded to the nearest coin per payout; Paid then doubles the result. Level 1 = 1×, level 5 = 2×, level 10 = 3.25×.

The setup hides controls that do not belong to the selected mode, including individual bot portraits in tournament mode. The full bracket is an in-flow, full-width section with all rounds, future winner/loser feeds and completed matches. Scroll horizontally to view every round. The View full bracket button jumps there, and Back to board returns to the game. Watching continues until explicitly paused. Default spectator and local BvB pace is two seconds per move, adjustable from one to five seconds. Only one tournament bot game may be actively watched at a time. Board colors are individually adjustable and Black play flips board orientation. The board no longer inherits generic theme button styling. Real browser rendering and Worker CPU usage still need verification.

## Chess Academy
Learn Chess has eight lessons, 25 legal board exercises, two guided practice matches (8 and 12 accepted player turns or an earlier victory), and five questions per lesson. Passing requires 80%; best grades and attempt counts persist, and quiz retakes shuffle choices. Coaching uses legal-move evaluation, one-reply material/terminal checks and opening heuristics, not a full grandmaster engine. Guest/emulator practice is temporary; verified live accounts use the existing chess Worker endpoint with a separate server-owned course document. Eight passing grades grant Professor Squiggles atomically; repeated requests cannot grant twice. Academy practice awards no coins or Elo. Chess page only displays trophy requirements. Cryptic axolotl clues remain on mystery collection cards. Browser and Cloudflare CPU timing remain unverified.

## Chess School: Associate’s
The new school reuses the chess game area and existing legal engine. Four degree tiers are displayed; only the Associate’s is implemented. There are 15 sequential lessons, worked board examples, optional history, legal exercises, two coached labs and graded quizzes (80% to pass). The final quiz has ten questions. Progress and rewards for verified live users are server-authoritative under privateGames/chess-school-associates; the legacy eight-lesson academy document and rewards are retained, but the expanded curriculum starts at lesson one. The legacy endpoint remains compatible during rollout. Rewards are two milestone medals and an in-game Associate’s diploma in the Trophies collection, plus Professor Squiggles if not already owned. No paid membership or coin reward is required. Guest/emulator study is temporary and does not grant collectibles. Standard chess stalemate is explicitly taught as a draw, alongside the site's variant. History is optional; reference: https://handbook.fide.com/chapter/e012023. Deploy Worker before frontend, check existing Firestore IAM access and approved game origin; no new rules, migrations or secrets. Run node --test workers/cd-store/tests/chess-school.test.mjs. The degree is a fictional game award, not an accredited qualification.

### Associate’s final exam
After all 15 lesson quizzes are passed, the final exam unlocks. The learner plays White against Professor Squiggles at fixed bot difficulty 1, from a full starting position, with no coaching, move rejections based on strategy, or takebacks. The existing game engine and house rules determine the winner. A loss or resignation permits unlimited retakes with lesson grades retained. The active exam is saved and resumable; the diploma and professor reward require a server-confirmed win as well as passing coursework. Module medals retain their original milestones. No Elo or coin rewards are awarded for exam matches.

### In-game school navigation
Chess School is a tab of pages/chess.html, not a separate game. The legacy learn-chess.html redirects to chess.html#school; Board Games lists only Axolotl Chess. Degree selection is independent of the Associate’s classroom; Professor Squiggles is assigned only to the Associate’s, and future instructors are unassigned. Switching to school pauses normal bot timers without resetting matches, and returning resumes normal scheduled play. Course state persists as before. No backend changes required for the navigation itself.

### Begin practice troubleshooting
The school validates the Worker response against the 15-lesson/exam contract before using saved state; legacy eight-lesson responses are rejected with a deploy instruction. Loading, save errors and timeouts appear in the classroom beside the action, with retry and explicit unsaved practice. Unsaved practice never writes rewards or overwrites saved course progress. Begin Practice hides the worked example and scrolls to the interactive exercise after the state transition. Existing live members must deploy the Worker from this branch before saved lessons can begin.

### Diploma and medal collections
School medals are stored under users/{uid}/medals. The sole chess diploma is users/{uid}/diplomas/chess, with rank 1 (Associate’s), 2 (Bachelor’s), 3 (Master’s), or 4 (PhD). Only rank 1 is currently awarded; higher degree courses are not implemented. The server helper uses conditional updates and never downgrades a higher diploma. Old branch trophy awards remain stored, are hidden from the trophy grid, and are recognized in the appropriate new tab; loading a previously completed school backfills new records based on server-owned progress. Medals and diplomas cannot be bought or written by clients. **Publish the updated security/firestore.rules before using the new tabs**, then deploy the Worker and frontend. No new IAM permission is expected; check existing Firestore read/write access. No deletion of existing awards or D1 migration is required.
