# Chess and board games

Chess uses an original king-capture variant, not FIDE chess. Pieces move normally (including en passant, castling without check restrictions, and promotion). The king can be captured, and a side with no available move loses. To prevent endless matches, 100 quiet half-moves or 400 total plies trigger material then mobility adjudication (last mover wins a tie). The ten levels use a seeded, bounded JavaScript search; level 1 plays random moves. Bot Elo values are estimated starting values, updated per account by rated PvB matches, not verified FIDE ratings. Online PvP Elo update logic exists but networking is not implemented.

Single elimination: eight entrants (you + seven distinct owned game-earned axolotls), three rounds to win. 3GG: every entrant plays at least three games, with winners and losers bracket feeds, a championship, and a reset final if the undefeated finalist loses game 16. A player losing their opening two games still has a third game in the championship path. The bracket can contain 16 or 17 matches; see [eight-team 3GG reference](https://www.printyourbrackets.com/3-game-guarantee/8-team-seeded-3-game-guarantee.html). Each pending bot match can be watched as actual server-validated chess, one move at a time, or simulated instantly using the seeded equal-rating outcome model. The bracket waits until every match in its current wave is complete. White, Black or Random may be selected for the player; tournament difficulty is independently selected from 1–10. Watched matches preserve their board on the server and do not affect the player's Elo. Local PvP, PvB and BvB practice do not award collectibles or change account ratings. Signed-in rated PvB and tournaments are verified by the live Worker, and a saved active tournament resumes. Rated matches now earn base capture coins: pawn 1, knight/bishop 3, rook 5, queen 9, king 10; win 20; tournament championship 50. Capture earnings are capped at 60 per game and credited when a match ends naturally, including a loss. Resigning forfeits pending capture earnings. Paid membership doubles the verified payout at credit time, using the matching preview/live staff override. Bot-only and local practice matches never award coins. All game rewards are server-only; the existing privateGames and axolotl rules already prevent client grants. The six collectible unlock rules are in chess-core.mjs; Chess Master requires a difficulty-10 tournament championship.

Test local practice without a Worker. For rated play, after reviewing and testing the current branch, deploy the live Worker using `cd workers/cd-store` then `npx wrangler deploy --config wrangler.live.toml`. Existing verified accounts on approved localhost/preview origins can use it. No new Firestore rules or Blaze needed. Run `node --test workers/cd-store/tests/chess.test.mjs` before deployment. Browser and Cloudflare CPU timing require real testing; high bot levels use bounded search, not Stockfish.

Difficulty scaling: base earnings are multiplied by (difficulty + 3) / 4, rounded to the nearest coin per payout; Paid then doubles the result. Level 1 = 1×, level 5 = 2×, level 10 = 3.25×.

The setup hides controls that do not belong to the selected mode, including individual bot portraits in tournament mode. The full bracket dialog shows future winner/loser feeds as well as completed matches. Watching pauses while the bracket is open. Default spectator and local BvB pace is two seconds per move, adjustable from one to five seconds. Only one tournament bot game may be actively watched at a time. Board colors are individually adjustable and Black play flips board orientation. The board no longer inherits generic theme button styling. Real browser rendering and Worker CPU usage still need verification.
