# Banner ad integration: phase 1

The AdSense unit is PTG Universal Banner, publisher ca-pub-9671323559975419, slot 8870216368. The existing ads.txt remains unchanged.

## Test before merging
On a local emulator or hosted preview, sign in as an active administrator or owner and visit Admin > Accounts > My membership. Switch to Free: the AD HERE test banner appears directly below the toggle. Switch to Paid: it disappears. The same membership state controls one nonsticky mock banner per eligible page: Home immediately below the introduction and outside its tabs; browse pages near the intro or between music playback and catalog; individual album/song/chapter pages below content; all seven games below their entire game area; all four storefronts below shopping content; and challenges below their content. Checkout/receipt states and modal dialogs suppress the placeholder, as does focus within a challenge form. A preview displays the placeholder for guests and confirmed Free accounts; the production hostname displays it only for staff who explicitly select Free view. Paid and unresolved memberships never display it. Health, surveys, profile, admin pages (except the dedicated demonstration), documents including shared readers, settings, legal pages, game editors, errors and 404 are excluded. Gameplay has only an out-of-game-area banner, not an in-game interruption.

Run `node tests/banner-ads.test.cjs`. The Advertisements control panel now requires the updated Firestore rules. Run `firebase deploy --only firestore:rules --project music-and-poetry` from the branch when using Live Firebase, or restart the demo emulator with the new rules. No Worker, Stripe or Blaze changes.

## Before activating real ads
Real ads are not implemented or loaded in this phase. Confirm AdSense site approval, disable Auto ads, arrange applicable consent/privacy controls (including a Google-certified CMP where required), review age eligibility, update the privacy notice, and review the owner-controlled settings and per-page configuration before implementing a real provider loader. Never reward banner clicks or reload banners to generate impressions. Keep real ad requests off previews/local development and ensure Paid membership suppresses requests, not merely the displayed element. Review gameplay ad placements separately.

## Troubleshooting
If banners do not appear, confirm the current branch is deployed and Admin > Accounts reports Free (not Paid or Unavailable). The auth dialog exists in the DOM even when its outer wrapper is hidden: only a visibly rendered dialog suppresses a mock banner. On localhost, use the same Firebase environment and account as the membership toggle. Production mock banners remain restricted to staff testing Free; guests and normal Free users can test on previews.

The mock module starts independently of shared navigation, and logs `[PTG test banner]` with the current visibility reason (visible, paid, checking-membership, membership-error, dialog-open, checkout-active, form-active, or not-eligible). No account details are logged.

## Admin → Advertisements (phase 2)
The new tab stores two isolated public, non-sensitive settings documents: advertisementSettings/live for the production origin and advertisementSettings/preview for all previews (local emulators are additionally isolated by project). Only the active owner can save; staff can view. New or missing documents use test banners on, real/interstitial/rewarded intentions off, 5 completed levels and 10 minutes, all 36 placements enabled. A failed server read hides mock banners rather than ignoring a saved disabled setting. A master mock switch and individual page switches take effect on all open eligible pages after the server snapshot. The admin membership demo follows the mock master switch. A preview setting does not carry over to production; configure each separately.

The three planned real-ad switches only save configuration. No real provider script exists and no ad is shown even if all switches are checked. The pure gameBreakEligible helper requires Free membership, an approved natural break, both configured thresholds, and the two intended flags; it does not yet attach to any game. Rewarded ads require voluntary opt-in, provider completion and a trusted idempotent reward path; never award Star Coins based on a client-reported click or simulated ad. No reward endpoint is implemented.

## Provider and privacy launch checklist
1. Confirm the AdSense site is Ready and leave Auto ads off. In [AdSense](https://adsense.google.com/), open Privacy & messaging, create/publish the European regulations message for the verified domain, and review applicable US state privacy messages, including opt-out choices and a way to reopen privacy preferences. See [Google CMP requirements](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/13554116) and [CMP setup](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/7670013). Confirm the consent integration before any ad requests; consent refusal or uncertainty must follow the applicable provider/legal requirements, not be assumed to permit personalized ads.
2. Apply separately for [H5 Games Ads](https://developers.google.com/ad-placement/docs/signup). Ordinary banners are not rewarded ads. Use natural game breaks and test provider callbacks only after access is granted.
3. Draft privacy-policy changes for owner review: explain Google advertising, cookies/device identifiers/IP and ad measurement, provider data use, consent/opt-out and privacy choices; link [Google partner-site data use](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/). Review age eligibility and regional requirements; keep health data, survey answers, account data, documents and purchase screens outside ad targeting and placement. Do not publish this as a claim of active ads yet. Before launch update the actual notice and any policy-version/acceptance handling consistently. No consent or policy compliance is asserted by this draft.
4. Test the settings, membership, privacy choices, provider integration and layouts on previews, without real impressions. Only then implement the production provider gate and enable it in the verified environment.
