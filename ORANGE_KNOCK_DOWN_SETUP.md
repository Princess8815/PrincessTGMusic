# Orange Knock Down
Fifth Fruity Paradice game. Original illustrated slingshot orchard. Mouse/touch pull from the launcher or accessible angle/power controls. Fixed-step projectiles, destructible branches, falling oranges, increasing procedural orchard size and tougher branches. Sniper selects any remaining orange and always knocks it down.
- Marina has the level's free shot allowance (limited allowance scaled to structural durability). Aqualily splits into three; Bulky deals triple branch damage; Sploder bursts nearby branches/oranges; Sniper guarantees one orange. Specials are extra throws, three each once per account, 100 Star Coins for one additional use. Purchases persist; no refund on retry. 9,999 stored uses per type safety cap.
- Each newly cleared level earns 10 Star Coins once; free retries replenish only Marina. Procedural compact towers increase in height, material hardness and obstacle complexity; numerical level limit 1,000,000. Orange Knock Down Mastery trophy is awarded on completion of level 1,000, including existing qualifying accounts on their next load. Merely reaching the level does not qualify.
- Fruiter rolls on each completed level, 1/10,000 initially times (1+0.25*(level-1)), probability capped at 1. Tangie rolls 1% on completed levels 11 and later. Tangie is the new collection reward; launch types are game power-ups, not new profile collectibles.
- Existing trusted Worker, privateGames/orangeKnockDown, atomic wallet writes, revision preconditions and idempotent retry; no new rules. Browser submits only launch inputs; server reconstructs result. Predictable layouts and deterministic physics permit solver bots; not proof of human play. No periodic polling.
- Fullscreen, mobile controls, persistent progress and pending request recovery. Homepage title and Open Graph metadata changed to PrincessTG Creations.
- Run node --test workers/cd-store/tests/orange-knock-down.test.mjs. Deploy: cd workers/cd-store && npx wrangler deploy --config wrangler.live.toml.

- New levels use layout version 2: load-bearing supports, glass (3+), two-hit brick barricades (5+), stone (8+), indestructible reflective backstop (12+), steel (16+), angled reflectors and taller walls (20+). A lost support drops connected structures and their fruit. Existing saved version-1 levels finish using the legacy map, then advance to the new structure engine without losing inventory or rewards. The centered Next level button is inside the victory overlay.

- Layout version 3 rotates ten structural puzzle families and varies positions/materials by level and seed. Previous version 1/2 boards stay intact until next level. New milestones: spring bumpers level 24, reinforced alloy level 30, chain-reaction crates level 36. Feature progress uses confirmed highest level cleared, measured within the current milestone interval. Mystery silhouettes reveal names, color and explanations when unlocked; the win overlay shows the discovery or next progress. No additional requests or storage fields for unlocks.

- Layout version 4 adds gold horizontal trampolines at level 27. They boost descending shots upward with bounded speed and preserve forward motion, remain indestructible, and have their own mystery/reveal icon. Older maps keep their version until the next level. No change to coin or collectible rewards.

- Layout version 5 allows bounce pads at arbitrary angles with two-sided mirror reflection and a capped 1.35x speed boost (minimum 20, maximum 32). Level 27 introduces a slanted bank; later levels add relay and back-bank pads. Existing version 4 horizontal pads retain their original behavior and saved geometry until advancing. No changes to rewards.

- Fullscreen is arena-only: Back returns to the regular game menu without resetting the attempt. Compact read-only arena counters remain; feature cards, shop, sliders, help and other panels are hidden. A compact launcher tray at the bottom of fullscreen displays Marina and all four specials with remaining charges; the selected launcher is highlighted. Purchases stay in the menu. The 16:9 board stays fully visible (letterboxed in portrait). Marina reaching zero while oranges remain ends the attempt on both client and server, regardless of spare special charges; retry restores the current level's Marina allowance only. Clearing the final orange with Marina's last throw still wins. No Firestore rule changes; redeploy the Worker for the updated loss rule.

## Grid level studio
- Open Level editor from Orange Knock Down. All ten material tools and oranges are available to every designer. 20-pixel grid, numeric rotation (1-degree precision), length increments, explicit support and orange links, undo/redo, local autosaved draft and a guest-accessible Test level button. Test plays with the same physics and a fresh temporary special inventory; no game endpoint calls, coins, trophies, collectibles or campaign progress. Save & share requires an active signed-in Live Firebase account. Save as new copies another creator's design. Community levels can be browsed and loaded using share codes. Authors and staff can edit/delete community levels.
- Each design specifies 1–50 Marina throws and the minimum remaining Marina throws for two/three stars. Stars are performance ratings, not currency; one star is always awarded for a clear. Special throws do not reduce Marina remaining. Official first clear still awards 10 coins. A current level's rating is stored in its board until the next level.
- Admin > Orange Levels opens the official level editor and configures unlock levels for each mechanic (wood fixed at 1, angled reflectors cannot precede ordinary reflectors). Official publication validates the material schedule. New attempts entering a level use the published map if compatible; otherwise procedural fallback. A level already entered keeps its existing geometry, including on retry. Milestone settings are snapshotted when entering each new level. Changing the schedule can invalidate earlier official publications; review those levels after edits. No automatic guarantee that a custom level is solvable; test before publishing.
- Existing server-only Firestore collections orangeConfig, orangeOfficialLevels and orangeCommunityLevels are accessed through the live Worker, using existing service-account permissions. No new client rules. Public browse returns up to 20 level names at once. Official publish/milestone operations require verified active staff. Current campaign actions still use revision checks; the server never accepts a client-provided campaign map. The editor limit is 48 pieces and 32 oranges. Redeploy Worker and publish site together.

- Reset campaign on the game menu restarts at level 1 using the latest official map and milestone schedule after confirmation. Current progression resets; Star Coins, special inventory, trophies and collectibles remain. The server retains the lifetime highest rewarded level and prevents repeated coin/collectible rewards after resets. Replayed wins still award a performance rating and advance the campaign. The action is revision-checked and idempotent, and not available in practice mode.

- New gravity-enabled designs and new generated campaign maps settle blocks onto angled surfaces and ground, then settle oranges on blocks/fruit. Reflective/bounce fixtures stay anchored. Unsupported surviving pieces settle after each shot; broken fragments fall and can knock fruit down. Ground-reaching falling fruit count as cleared; oranges already on the ground remain targets. Final poses persist. Deterministic vertical settling is not a full rotating rigid-body solver; debris fades after landing. Saved non-gravity maps retain legacy physics.

- Level Studio now offers a visual material palette, pointer drag/drop, a live snapped ghost showing actual gravity landing, boundary clamping, and keyboard nudging. Manual support fields are no longer needed in new gravity designs. Loading an old design into the editor converts its draft to gravity on the next save/test (previous published maps remain unchanged).

- Official starter levels 2–4 are bundled as editable wood-only gravity layouts (Twin Timber Towers, Orchard Staircase, Timber Fortress). Firestore official publications override the bundled defaults. Staff can load/edit these directly from the official editor and save to the same number. Default glass unlock moves to level 5 alongside brick; explicitly saved milestone settings remain authoritative. Existing attempts retain their snapshots; reset campaign to revisit the new layouts. All three have deterministic Marina-only solution replays.

- Gravity boards now use deterministic fixed-step angular/linear impulses and shape contacts during the shot. Fresh blocks rebound Marina even when shattered; an already damaged block may be pushed through at sufficient speed. Fragments fall and collide, displaced blocks disappear after grounding, and hit oranges move before clearing on the ground. Initial foundations remain, fixed ricochet fixtures stay anchored, and Sniper keeps its guaranteed clear. Saved positions include x/y/angle. Legacy non-gravity boards retain prior rules. This is an approximate game physics solver, not a general-purpose rigid-body engine.

- Contact-connected structures wake on impact; disconnected structures remain asleep unless struck by moving bodies. Surviving debris is retained on shelves across shots, and loose fragments disappear on the ground. Admin unlock settings and official map definitions are untouched.

- Levels 5–9 are editable handcrafted wood/glass designs. Starter templates respect the saved milestone schedule; when glass is still locked their glass pieces temporarily become wood. Official saved designs retain precedence when compatible. No unlock values are changed. Glass shatters into 3–5 physical shards depending on length; rendering uses jagged translucent polygons, collision uses approximate rectangular bodies. All five starter layouts have Marina-only regression replays.

- Music and Sound toggles are separate, available in the upper right in normal and fullscreen gameplay, and stored per browser. An original synthesized orchard loop and launch, wood/glass, bounce, orange, explosion, victory and defeat effects use Web Audio (no external audio assets). Browser autoplay restrictions require an initial interaction; audio is muted while the tab is hidden. Game reward toast sounds respect the game Sound setting via silent visual toasts. Browser audio and mobile placement need live verification.

- Level selection allows any level through the current campaign frontier (cleared + 1). Selecting a level resets that attempt using the latest compatible official layout, without refunding specials. Replays cannot duplicate economic rewards. Best stars are server-verified and stored in 30-level pages, with a lifetime total updated atomically with game and wallet. Reset campaign preserves lifetime best stars, but resets the accessible frontier. Older saves receive one star per lifetime cleared level plus their known current winning rating; earlier exact scores were not stored and cannot be reconstructed. The picker reads pages on demand.

- Sound now follows material-specific contacts, including falling debris and ground impacts, with stereo position and impact-strength scaling when supported. Victory plays one chime for each earned rating star followed by a level-up melody for newly advanced campaign progress or a replay victory melody. The existing Sound toggle mutes all game effects. Level picker and best-star totals are available in the game menu and victory overlay; previous progress migration uses the known current rating and a one-star minimum for older clears. No new client Firestore permissions are required.

## Campaign rebuild (levels 1–250)
Fixed level-number seeds generate 250 editable layouts with independent bays, reinforcement and progressive structures. Hard: 24,34,...,244; super-hard: 29,39,...,249. Default schedule: wood 1, glass 5, brick 10, stone 15, panels 20, steel 30, angled reflectors 40, spring 50, trampoline 75, alloy 100, crates 200. Saved administrator schedules remain authoritative. Published staff overrides remain in place; selecting/replaying a level reloads its current design. Older in-progress maps keep their physics snapshots. New maps have physics version 2: wood 2 HP, glass 1, brick/stone 3, steel 4, alloy 5, greater mass and friction, smaller physical impact impulses, no teleporting damaged blocks, and four world substeps per frame. Foundations no longer vanish merely because they take damage or shift slightly; fallen or toppled pieces disappear after grounding. New editor designs use the same physics.

- Campaign reset now clears all star ratings and total stars through an atomic generation increment. Old rating pages are ignored and overwritten as players earn new ratings; first-clear coin and collectible eligibility remains lifetime-based. New default levels use tighter Marina allowances (2–7). Existing published custom maps retain their designer's allowance, and existing in-progress snapshots retain their layout; select/replay or reset to load the revised defaults. Victory previews upcoming hard/super-hard levels, with red/purple Next level buttons.

- Glass fracture audio now uses three generated broadband crack-and-shard samples with irregular cascading impacts and inharmonic resonance; it remains spatial, strength-scaled, and controlled by the Sound toggle. No external audio assets or requests.
