Will It Be Worth It

Song details page

Description

This song is a callout to those who support political movements and parties that actively harm vulnerable people while dismissing the human cost. It asks a simple but painful question: what was the price of that support, and was it ever truly worth it? For those whose rights, safety, and futures are treated as bargaining chips, the damage is never theoretical.
