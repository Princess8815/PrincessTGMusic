Description

“I Survived What You Are Crying Through” is an emotional alt-rock survival anthem about speaking to someone trapped in pain, fear, and exhaustion from the perspective of someone who made it through. With chant-like hooks, layered background vocals, and a message of endurance, the song turns suffering into proof that survival is possible.
