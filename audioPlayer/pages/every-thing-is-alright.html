Every Thing Is Alright

Description

A song about surviving hatred, betrayal, and pain while refusing to be broken by it. The lyrics follow a journey from resilience and self-discovery to open defiance, transforming emotional wounds into strength, unity, and resistance. It centers on perseverance, reclaiming identity, and standing unshaken against those who tried to silence or destroy you.
