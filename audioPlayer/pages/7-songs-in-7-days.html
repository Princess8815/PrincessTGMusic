7 songs in 7 days

Song details page

Description

Written as the final chapter of a personal seven songs in seven days challenge, this song captures the emotional weight of pushing through doubt, exhaustion, pain, and self-discovery to reach the finish line. Each verse reflects a different day of the journey, turning moments of struggle into music, until the final track becomes both a celebration of survival and a message to anyone fighting their own hard days: keep going.
